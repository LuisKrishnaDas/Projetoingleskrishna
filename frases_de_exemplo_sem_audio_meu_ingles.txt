MEU INGLÊS — FRASES DE EXEMPLO SEM ÁUDIO PRÓPRIO
Auditoria do ZIP enviado. As expressões principais têm áudio cadastrado; estas frases de exemplo não têm botão de reprodução na aula.

1. hello — olá
   Hello! My name is Ana.

2. good morning — bom dia
   Good morning, everyone!

3. please — por favor
   A coffee, please.

4. thank you — obrigado(a)
   Thank you for your help.

5. I am — eu sou / estou
   I am happy today.

6. my name — meu nome
   My name is Lucas.

7. from — de (origem)
   I am from Brazil.

8. friend — amigo(a)
   She is my friend.

9. eat — comer
   I eat breakfast at home.

10. drink — beber
   I drink water every day.

11. work — trabalhar
   I work in the morning.

12. sleep — dormir
   I sleep at night.

13. water — água
   Can I have some water?

14. coffee — café
   I would like a coffee.

15. where — onde
   Where is the bathroom?

16. help — ajuda / ajudar
   Can you help me, please?

17. actually — na verdade
   Actually, I changed my mind.

18. although — embora
   Although it was late, we kept working.

19. already — já
   I have already paid the bill.

20. enough — suficiente
   I don't have enough time today.

21. even though — mesmo que / apesar de
   She came even though she was sick.

22. get used to — acostumar-se com
   It takes time to get used to a new schedule.

23. look forward to — aguardar com expectativa
   I look forward to meeting you.

24. figure out — descobrir, entender
   I can't figure out this problem.

   Don't give up now.

28. turn down — recusar
   He decided to turn down the offer.
